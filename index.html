Live Safety Protocols

Mandatory Safe-Isolation Rules

Every technician follows calibrated verification procedures before contact with any electrical conductor:

  • 1. Identify supply source & lock off with personal padlock
  • 2. Test proving unit with calibrated voltage detector
  • 3. Test circuits: Phase-to-Phase, Phase-to-Neutral, Phase-to-Earth
  • 4. Re-test proving unit to confirm tester integrity
Customer Safety Notice Never attempt DIY diagnostic testing on live service panels. In case of sparking or burnt smell, switch off the main switch if safe to reach, or evacuate immediately.
