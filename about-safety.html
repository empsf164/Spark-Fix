Operational Safeguards

Field Safety Standards

We maintain an uncompromising protocol before a screwdriver touches a terminal. Here is how our certified technicians safeguard every environment:

We never work on live circuits without documented technical justification. Every circuit is isolated, locked off with an individual padlock and danger tag, verified with a calibrated voltage indicator, and proven dead against a proving unit.

Technicians wear rated dielectric safety footwear, 1000V-rated insulated hand tools, flame-resistant workwear, safety eyewear, and arc-rated face shields when interfacing with high-energy commercial distribution panels.

Before energization, thorough dead testing is conducted: Continuity of protective conductors (R1+R2), ring final continuity, and insulation resistance tested at 500V DC to ensure zero cable degradation or fire risks.
Customer Safety Directives

Electrical faults are severe fire and life-safety hazards. Follow these responsible protocols:

  • Never touch exposed wires: Treat all unknown conductors as energized until verified by a certified professional.
  • Avoid water near electrical equipment: If a basement or utility room is flooded, do not step into water to reach the panel.
  • Do not perform DIY panel repairs: Bypassing circuit breakers or replacing fuses with higher amperages creates extreme fire hazards.
  • Active Smoke or Fire: Immediately evacuate all occupants, isolate mains if safely accessible from a distance, and call local municipal emergency services (911/999/112).
