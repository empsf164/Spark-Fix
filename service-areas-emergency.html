Emergency Electrical Assistance

Electrical failures, burning odors, or severe sparking pose catastrophic hazards. Follow our responsible 5-step triage process.

Clear Operating Distinction: An emergency enquiry submission initiates rapid technical triage. It does not constitute guaranteed immediate dispatch. Arrangements are confirmed subject to specialist technician proximity, safety risk assessment, and severe weather conditions. Never delay contacting municipal emergency services (911/999/112) if active fire or immediate physical danger is present.

Attendance window and dispatch arrangements are formally confirmed based on availability.

WHAT NOT TO DO (CRITICAL HAZARDS)
  • DO NOT touch exposed conductors or severed cables: Assume any loose wire carries lethal voltage until proven dead.
  • DO NOT step into water near electrical equipment: Water conducts electricity; if a utility area or cellar is flooded, stay back.
  • DO NOT repeatedly force tripped breakers back on: A breaker trips to prevent electrical fires from fault currents. Repeated reset causes explosion.
  • DO NOT attempt DIY electrical repairs: Live mains power can cause lethal ventricular fibrillation within 0.1 seconds.
WHAT TO DO (RESPONSIBLE PROTOCOL)
  • Evacuate immediately if smoke or fire is detected: Move all family members or employees to a safe outdoor assembly area.
  • Switch off the main isolator ONLY IF safe to reach: If the panel is dry, clear of sparks, and safe to approach, turn off the master switch.
  • Call Local Fire / Emergency Services (911/999/112): In case of active fire or burning smell inside walls, alert public first responders.
  • Contact SPARK FIX for safe technical coordination: Call our hotline to initiate intake and request certified technician attendance.
