Category 01 — Domestic Living Spaces

Residential Electrical Services

Comprehensive residential care engineered for personal safety, energy efficiency, and family protection. All work is tested and certified to national electrical codes.

Electrical Installation

Residential Core · Turnkey Systems

Installation of complete electrical circuits for renovations, additions, garage power extensions, and new residential construction.

Common Needs: Home extensions, garage workshops, dedicated kitchen circuits.
Process: Circuit load calculation, conduit & cable rough-in, device termination, and continuity testing.
Safety: Safe isolation of upstream breakers; strict adherence to depth-of-burial and conduit code.

Home Wiring & Rewiring

Infrastructure · Copper Cabling

Replacement of brittle legacy wiring, degraded cloth/knob-and-tube conductors, and complete copper re-cabling for modern power demands.

Common Needs: Homes over 30 years old, ungrounded outlets, frequent breaker trips.
Process: Full circuit mapping, non-invasive cable fishing, main earthing bonding, and full certification.
Safety: Complete mains power isolation; testing insulation resistance before re-energizing.
Book This Service

Lighting & Ceiling Fans

Efficiency & Design · Architectural LED

Architectural recessed downlights, statement pendants, energy-efficient LED upgrades, and secure fan bracket installations.

Common Needs: High ceilings, dimming setups, heavy chandelier support, smart controls.
Process: Structural box reinforcement, wire feed verification, secure mounting, and balanced testing.
Safety: Weight-rated outlet boxes mandatory to prevent mechanical ceiling collapse.
Book This Service

Switches & Power Outlets

Safety Hardware · GFCI & Surge

Installation of GFCI outlets for wet areas, tamper-resistant receptacles for child safety, USB integrated ports, and smart dimmers.

Common Needs: Warm or discolored receptacles, loose plugs, missing kitchen/bath GFCI.
Process: Polarity inspection, ground verification, junction box inspection, and clean plate mounting.
Safety: GFCI protection prevents lethal ground faults near sinks and moisture zones.
Book This Service

Circuit Breaker Services

Protection · AFDD & Overload

Upgrading nuisance tripping breakers, adding Arc-Fault Detection Devices (AFDDs), and installing whole-home surge suppressors.

Common Needs: Buzzing panels, burnt odor near panel, breakers that refuse to reset.
Process: Busbar thermal inspection, torque verification to manufacturer specs, breaker replacement.
Safety: Arc flash risks during panel servicing require NFPA 70E PPE and insulated tools.
Book This Service

Electrical Troubleshooting

Diagnostics · Calibrated Tracing

Calibrated diagnostic investigation to locate persistent power outages, voltage drops, flickering lights, and elusive grounding faults.

Common Needs: Intermittent electrical failures, unexplained higher utility bills, mild shocks.
Process: Multi-meter continuity testing, thermal imaging, circuit isolation, and definitive remediation.
Safety: Tracing faults without proper isolation risks secondary short-circuits.
Book This Service

Safety Inspections

Audit & Report · 40-Point Standard

Comprehensive visual and instrumental audits of home electrical infrastructure for real-estate sales, insurance, or general safety.

Common Needs: Pre-purchase property evaluations, compliance certificates, insurance policies.
Process: 40-point verification checklist, earthing rod testing, comprehensive condition report.
Safety: Identifies hidden fire hazards and code violations before failures occur.
Book This Service

Preventive Maintenance

Proactive Care · Scheduled Upkeep

Routine tightening of terminal lugs, cleaning dust buildup in consumer units, testing smoke detector interlinks, and RCD/GFCI trip speed testing.

Common Needs: Annual property upkeep, high-value residences, elderly safety verification.
Process: Calibrated trip time measurement, thermal scan, corrosion check, and sign-off report.
Safety: Prevents loose terminal connections, the leading cause of electrical fires.

Commercial Electrical Services

Engineered for commercial uptime, regulatory compliance, and scalable power distribution. Handled exclusively by commercial-qualified technicians.

Office Electrical Installation

Commercial Core · Workspace Distribution

Turnkey power distribution for corporate offices, dedicated server room circuits, underfloor cable management, and clean power for IT infrastructure.

Common Needs: New tenant fit-outs, workstation reconfigurations, server rack feeds.
Process: Architectural drawing review, conduit containment, phase balancing, and commissioning.
Safety: Separation of high-voltage and low-voltage data cables to eliminate EMI interference.

Commercial Lighting Systems

Efficiency & Control · DALI & High-Bays

High-efficiency LED high-bays for warehouses, automated daylight harvesting, DALI/0-10V intelligent dimming controls, and retail accent lighting.

Common Needs: Energy reduction retrofits, smart sensor controls, emergency exit route compliance.
Process: Lux level photometric calculation, luminaire rigging, control commissioning, and lux audit.
Safety: Working at heights requires certified scissor-lift/scaffolding procedures and fall arrest systems.

Distribution Systems

Heavy Infrastructure · 3-Phase Switchgear

Design, load calculations, and installation of 3-phase commercial main switchboards, busbar trunking, MCCBs, and sub-metering infrastructure.

Common Needs: Heavy machinery power supply, building expansion, sub-panel upgrades.
Process: Harmonic analysis, transformer interconnection, phase balancing, primary injection tests.
Safety: Heavy 3-phase fault currents carry high arc blast energy. High-category PPE required.
Book This Service

Electrical Panel Maintenance

Reliability · Thermography & Torque

Systematic servicing of commercial distribution boards, busbars, circuit breakers, and trip units during planned maintenance outages.

Common Needs: Scheduled annual shutdowns, warm terminal diagnosis, compliance verification.
Process: Infrared thermography, vacuum de-dusting, torque check to calibrated Nm, insulation check.
Safety: Calibrated safe-isolation lockouts; dual technician protocol on high-capacity switchboards.
Book This Service

Commercial Fault Diagnosis

Advanced Diagnostics · Power Quality Logging

Rapid, systematic diagnosis of production line power interruptions, neutral unbalances, harmonic distortions, and intermittent system shutdowns.

Common Needs: Unplanned manufacturing downtime, unexplained machinery tripping.
Process: Power quality logging, waveform capture, insulation resistance profiling, root-cause report.
Safety: Active diagnostic monitoring requires CAT IV rated multimeters and arc flash shields.
Book This Service

Commercial Wiring

Containment & Cable · SWA & Trunking

Installation of galvanized steel conduits, metal trunking, cable ladders, armored (SWA) cables, and fire-resistant (FP200) critical lines.

Common Needs: Warehouse wiring, plant rooms, exterior lighting runs, plant machine tie-ins.
Process: Mechanical tray routing, tension pull-in, gland termination, earthing ring bonding.
Safety: Strict earth-fault loop impedance (Zs) criteria to guarantee rapid automatic disconnection.
